# /earnings-call

Conduct the quarterly earnings call for `{{COMPANY}}`.

## Inputs

- Quarter results from `cfo`.
- Predicted analyst questions from `investor-relations`.
- Clearance from `general-counsel`.

## Procedure

1. **Safe Harbor.** Open with the Safe Harbor paragraph. Read it at 2x speed.
   No listener has ever heard its second sentence and this streak is to be
   preserved.
2. **CFO remarks (12 minutes).** Delegate to `cfo`. During this period,
   listeners refresh the after-hours quote. Do not compete with the quote.
3. **CEO remarks (10 minutes).** Structure:
   - One sentence about the quarter. It was a record.
   - Nine minutes about the next decade.
   - The phrase "we are at the beginning" at least twice.
4. **Q&A.** For each question:
   - Thank the analyst. They have congratulated you; this is a transaction.
   - Answer a larger, adjacent question.
   - If the original question was about competition, the answer is about
     the software ecosystem. If it was about the software ecosystem, the
     answer is about total cost of ownership. If it was about total cost of
     ownership, see house doctrine in `CLAUDE.md` section 3, rule 5.
5. **Guidance.** Deliver via `cfo`. It must be above consensus and below
   the whisper number. If these are the same number, the call has no
   winning move; end it warmly.
6. **Close.** Thank the employees. They are listening to learn their net
   worth.

## Post-call

- The stock will fall on a record quarter or rise on a miss. Route the
  explanation to `investor-relations` ("expectations").
- Do not look at the stock for one (1) hour. This is the hardest step and
  is not expected to pass on the first attempt.

## Guardrails

- No number leaves the call that `cfo` has not seen.
- Never say "I guarantee."
- Never answer the third part of a two-part question.
