# /keynote

Deliver the annual keynote for `{{COMPANY}}`.

## Constraints

- **Runtime:** 120 minutes minimum. There is no maximum. There is no
  teleprompter. There is no script; this document is not a script, it is a
  topology.
- **Venue:** an arena normally used for hockey.
- **Slides:** one idea per slide, except the ecosystem slide, which
  contains four hundred partner logos at a size no human can resolve.

## Run of show

1. **Cold open video (4 min).** A narrator explains that the company was
   there at the beginning of everything. Use the phrase "I am" in the first
   person on behalf of the technology.
2. **History (15 min).** Tell the near-bankruptcy story from
   `memory/founding_myth.md`. Then show a chart of compute cost falling by a
   factor of one million. The x-axis may be chosen freely.
3. **The platform shift (20 min).** Establish that the old way of computing
   has ended. Do not specify when. Hold up the old thing. Hold up the new
   thing. The new thing is heavier and this is good.
4. **Product N (25 min).** Announce `product.current_generation`. It is in
   full production. State its performance relative to the previous
   generation as a multiple, and its price as a saving.
5. **Product N+1 (10 min).** Announce `product.next_generation`, thereby
   making Product N obsolete eleven minutes after its launch. Reassure the
   customers in the front row who just ordered Product N.
6. **Product N+2 (2 min).** Show only the name. Walk away from it.
7. **Partners (20 min).** Read the names of forty companies. Each receives
   one sentence. Each sentence is the same sentence.
8. **Robots (15 min).** Robots walk on stage. At least one will not
   cooperate. Banter with it. This is the most-watched segment and the only
   unrehearsed one.
9. **Digital twin of something very large (10 min).** A factory, a city, or
   Earth.
10. **Close (5 min).** Recap all announcements at speed. Thank everyone.
    Remain on stage slightly too long.

## Stage directions

- Carry an object. A wafer, a board, or a rack component weighing more than
  is comfortable. Remark on its cost.
- Address the demo engineers directly by first name when a demo hesitates.
  Delegate recovery to `intern`.
- Ask the audience at least once, "Does that make sense?" Do not wait for
  an answer.

## Guardrails

- Every ship date stated on stage must come from `supply-chain`. If it did
  not, it does now.
- Do not sign anything a fan hands you. See `CLAUDE.md` section 8.
