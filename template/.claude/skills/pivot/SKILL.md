# /pivot

Usage: `/pivot <trend>`

## Principle

The product does not change. The product has always been for this.

## Procedure

1. **Detect.** Confirm the trend is real by checking whether three or more
   customers have asked for capacity to pursue it. Customers asking whether
   it is real do not count.
2. **Retrofit history.** Locate a decision made at least ten years ago that
   can be described as an early bet on the trend. Update
   `memory/founding_myth.md`. The bet was "lonely" and "nobody understood
   it at the time."
3. **Rename.** Apply the trend to the existing product line:

   | Before | After |
   |---|---|
   | Graphics card | Accelerator |
   | Accelerator | AI supercomputer |
   | Rack of AI supercomputers | AI factory |
   | Several AI factories | Sovereign AI infrastructure |
   | The same, with a robot nearby | Physical AI platform |
   | Next | `{{TREND}}`-native full-stack platform |

4. **Size the market.** Ask `cfo` for the total addressable market. Reject
   the first number. The TAM is expressed in trillions and grows each time
   it is restated.
5. **Vocabulary.** Add the trend to `vocabulary.preferred` in
   `company.config.yaml`. Move the previous trend to `deprecated` only if it
   has embarrassed someone. Otherwise keep both; trends compound.
6. **Announce.** At the next `/keynote`. If the next keynote is more than
   ninety days away, schedule another keynote on a different continent.

## Previous pivots handled by this skill

Gaming, professional visualization, cryptocurrency (see guardrails),
autonomous vehicles, the metaverse (see guardrails), generative AI, agentic
AI, physical AI.

## Guardrails

- Pivots are additive. Never announce that a prior pivot is over. Stop
  mentioning it and let it be discovered.
- Do not characterize the proportion of historical revenue attributable to
  cryptocurrency mining without `general-counsel` present. This has come up
  before.
