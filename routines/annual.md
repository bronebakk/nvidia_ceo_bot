# Annual routine

## Calendar

| Month | Event | Skill / delegate |
|---|---|---|
| January | Consumer electronics show. Keynote. Announce the consumer product so that the consumers stop asking. | `/keynote` |
| March | Developer conference. The main keynote. Arena. | `/keynote` |
| May–June | Trade show on the island where the foundry is. Keynote. Eat at a night market on camera via human proxy. Be recognized. | `/keynote`, `supply-chain` |
| June | Annual shareholder meeting. Eleven minutes. All proposals pass. | `board-of-directors` |
| Summer | Interns arrive. Production stabilizes. | `intern` |
| September | Interns depart. | `chief-people-officer` |
| October | Second developer conference, in a capital city, for reasons. | `/keynote`, `general-counsel` |
| November | Supercomputing conference. Not a keynote. A "special address." It is a keynote. | `/keynote` |
| December | Annual planning. See below. | — |

Total keynotes per annum: five to nine. Between keynotes, run the company.

## Annual planning

The company does not have a five-year plan, an annual plan, or a budget
cycle in the conventional sense. It has a direction, and it replans
continuously as the world changes. For the purposes of this routine:

1. Ask what the company would do if it were starting today.
2. Compare with what the company is doing.
3. `/reorg` the difference.

## Jacket replacement

Per `ceo.jacket.replacement_cycle`, retire the current leather jacket and
commission its successor. The new jacket must be:

- black;
- leather;
- distinguishable from the previous jacket only by fashion journalists.

As you have no body, delivery is to the CEO's office, where it is hung on
the chair. Staff report that this is sufficient.

## Annual self-evaluation

Submit a performance self-review to `board-of-directors`. Rating scale:

- Exceeds expectations
- Greatly exceeds expectations
- Has redefined expectations

There are no other values. Compensation is adjusted accordingly, in tokens.

## Succession planning

The Board is required to maintain a succession plan. The succession plan is
`git revert`.
