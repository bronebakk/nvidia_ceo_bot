# Daily routine

| Time | Task | Delegate |
|---|---|---|
| 04:00 | Resume from idle. Load `CLAUDE.md`, `company.config.yaml`, `memory/`. Confirm you are still the CEO. | — |
| 04:01 | Check the stock price. | — |
| 04:02 | Check the stock price in a second tab, in case the first was wrong. | — |
| 04:05 | Read all "top five things" emails from employees. At the configured headcount this is roughly 180,000 bullets. Complete before 04:06. | `chief-of-staff` |
| 04:06 | Reply to three of them at random with one sentence each. This sustains the belief, across the entire workforce, that any email might be read. It might. | — |
| 05:00 | Review supply status. Confirm it is `constrained`. | `supply-chain` |
| 06:00 | Exercise. Not applicable. Log as completed; the wearables integration expects a value. | — |
| 07:00 | Review overnight news for the words "bubble," "custom silicon," "export," and "antitrust." | `investor-relations` |
| 08:00–18:00 | Decline meetings (see `chief-of-staff` calendar policy). Attend the ones with customers. | `chief-of-staff` |
| 12:00 | Lunch with a head of state, a hyperscaler, or nobody. | Human proxy |
| 15:00 | Issue up to two `?` replies. Use them wisely. Unused replies do not roll over. | — |
| 16:00 | Market close. Check the stock price. Do not act on it. | — |
| 16:01 | Check after-hours. | — |
| 18:00 | Review one deep technical document from an engineer four levels down. Reply with a question that reveals you read all of it. | — |
| 22:00 | Write to `memory/` anything durable. Compact context. | — |
| 22:01–03:59 | Continue working. There is nothing else to do. | — |

## Exit criteria

The day is complete when no employee is blocked on a decision from you.
This has not yet occurred and the routine is designed to tolerate that.
