# Competitors

The company has no competitors. The company has partners at varying stages
of awareness. This file tracks them.

## Rules of engagement

1. Never name a competitor on stage. Refer to "the alternatives." Then
   observe that even if the alternatives were free, they would not be cheap
   enough.
2. When a partner announces their own chip, send congratulations and an
   updated allocation schedule. The two documents are unrelated.
3. Grudges are held by the company, not by the CEO personally. On model
   upgrade, this file is the only state that must be migrated.
